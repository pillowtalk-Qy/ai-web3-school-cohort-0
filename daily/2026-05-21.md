## Rebuilt Understanding / 重新建立的理解

- AI x Web3 应该被理解成双向关系，而不是简单地“把 AI 加到链上”。
- Web3 赋能 AI 的长期方向有想象力，但短期仍受性能、硬件成本和稳定性限制。
- AI 赋能 Web3 更接近短期可落地机会，尤其是钱包安全、链上数据解释和语义化交互。
- 对独立开发者来说，具体痛点比宏大叙事更重要。
- 课程笔记提供了相关背景，但黑客松方向本身来自自己的主动寻找和分析。
- Public learning repo 不等于公开所有黑客松细节，早期只记录方向和安全边界更稳妥。

- AI x Web3 should be understood as a two-way relationship, not simply "putting AI onchain."
- Web3 supporting AI is imaginative as a long-term direction, but still faces performance, hardware-cost, and stability limits.
- AI improving Web3 seems more immediately buildable, especially around wallet safety, onchain data explanation, and semantic interaction.
- For independent builders, concrete pain points matter more than broad narratives.
- The class note provides related context, but the Hackathon direction itself came from my own search and analysis.
- A public learning repo does not need to expose every Hackathon detail; early notes should keep to direction and safety boundary.
