## Hackathon Direction / 黑客松方向

今天对 AI Wallet Clear Intent Guard 的判断更清楚了。

不应该做：

- 完整 AI wallet。
- 自动签名工具。

```text

```

中文表达：

```text

```

最小 MVP 可以是四张 review card：

1. User intent card
2. Transaction facts card
3. Policy check card
4. x402-like payment route card

我的判断是：这个方向适合黑客松，因为它足够具体，demo 范围可控，又能连接课程强调的钱包工具、安全治理、agentic commerce 和 AI payment。

The Hackathon direction should not be a full AI wallet. A better scope is a pre-signing safety layer that compares user intent, deterministic transaction facts, policy constraints, and payment-route details before the user signs.

- AI wallet safety 需要三层一起看：agent identity / reputation、wallet authority / safeguards、payment routing / settlement openness。
- 黑客松项目需要能跑，也需要能说清楚为什么安全、为什么不自动越权、为什么有真实用户价值。

- The minimum safe AI x Web3 loop is not "AI controls the wallet." It is "AI explains and prepares, tools verify facts, humans keep signing authority, and the chain records the result."
- The concept cards are not basic repetition. They build shared language for the Hackathon direction.
- Cobo and Coinbase represent complementary references: one for authority and policy, the other for developer tooling and payment flow.
- AI wallet safety should be viewed across identity / reputation, wallet authority / safeguards, and payment routing / settlement openness.
