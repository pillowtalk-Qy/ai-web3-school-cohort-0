```text

-> user reviews on phone
-> approve or reject
```

English companion:

## Hackathon Direction / 黑客松方向

今天之前，`AI Wallet Clear Intent Guard` 还在几个入口之间摇摆：

```text
x402 payment request review
ERC-20 approve mismatch
authority package review
```

今天之后，方向变得更清楚：

```text
Candidate A: Packet / authority package review
Candidate B: x402 payment review with pre-sign facts
```

`Packet / authority package review` 的优势是：

- 更贴近 Cobo Wallet 产品里的真实权限机制。
- 天然有用户审批、有效期、次数、金额和合约范围。
- 可以很好地表达 human confirmation 和 audit proof。
- 更容易解释为什么需要 wallet policy boundary。

`x402 payment review` 的优势是：

- 更贴近支付场景。
- 更容易解释用户意图和 payment facts。
- 如果能拿到 pre-sign facts，就很适合做清楚的 review card。
- 可以和 `/policies/dry-run`、transaction record、webhook 形成审计闭环。

今天最大的变化不是换方向，而是把方向从概念变成了接口问题和产品结构问题：

```text
What facts can we get before signing?
What policy decision can we preview?
What proof can we show after execution?
```

English companion:

The demo direction is now more concrete. The key is no longer just "AI explains wallet risk"; it is whether the demo can show pre-sign facts, policy decisions, user approval, and post-execution proof.
