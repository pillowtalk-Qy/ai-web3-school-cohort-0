# Learning Profile

## Background

I have some prior experience with both AI and Web3.

For this cohort, I want to intentionally restart from the basics. This is a deliberate review process, not because everything is new to me, but because I want to rebuild the foundation in a more systematic way.

## Learning Mode

- Start from zero, even where I already have background knowledge.
- Follow the cohort rhythm instead of skipping ahead too quickly.
- Use daily notes to capture what I learned, what I misunderstood, and what I want to revisit.
- Use experiments to turn concepts into small working demos.
- Use Handbook feedback to contribute questions, corrections, and learning friction back to the community.

## Goals

- Rebuild my AI x Web3 foundation systematically.
- Strengthen my understanding of AI agents, context, tool use, and workflow design.
- Review Web3 foundations such as wallets, transactions, signatures, smart contracts, account abstraction, and security.
- Connect AI workflows with Web3 primitives such as wallets, identity, permissions, payments, governance, and verifiable records.
- Prepare for a Hackathon-ready idea by the end of the learning cycle.

## Public Boundary

This profile is intentionally lightweight because the repository may be public.

I will avoid publishing sensitive personal details, private wallet information, private work information, and anything that should not be part of a public learning record.
